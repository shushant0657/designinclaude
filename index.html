FAQ

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Claude in plain language. The skills produce HTML previews you open in a browser; developers can read them, but you never have to.

Which Claude plan do I need?

Any Claude plan that supports Skills (check Claude's settings for a Skills section). The skills also work in Claude Code.

Will it work with my design system?

That's the point. Brand-to-Skill reads your style guide, tokens, CSS or just screenshots of your app and turns them into a skill. Every other skill then follows your system — not a generic one.

I already use Impeccable, Hallmark or frontend-design. Do I need this?

They're great at general taste, and free. They don't know your product. This pack puts your own design system into Claude and covers the whole job around it — screens with every state, reviews, flows, handoff and team rollout. Use one general taste skill alongside it; when they disagree, your design system wins.

Does this replace Figma?

It can for a lot of work — exploring, prototyping, states, reviews and handoff. Many teams use both: design in Claude, then export what needs to live in Figma.

What's free and what's paid?

Levels 0 and 10 are free on this site — enough to encode your design system by hand. The pack adds Levels 25–100, the six skills that do every step for you on your own product, and eight bonus skills.

I don't have a design system yet. Is this for me?

Yes. Brand-to-Skill can start from references: pick 4–6 apps you love, say what you like in each, and it builds a starting system you then make your own. Levels 0 and 10 walk you through it.

What if it doesn't help?

Can my whole team use it?
